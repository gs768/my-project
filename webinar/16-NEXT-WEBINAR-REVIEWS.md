## 3. Draft outline of the reviews webinar (Gabriel to confirm and fill in from experience)
Same format as the LSA Playbook: about 60 minutes plus Q&A, Google's own words first, then field experience, then proof.

1. **Hook:** "Every agency tells you to get more reviews. Few will tell you which reviews Google counts, which it filters, and why a batch of 20 after a settlement can do nothing." (Gabriel to sharpen.)
2. **What Google says** (screenshots, captured like S1-S7): Google Business Profile help on how local results are ranked (relevance, distance, prominence; reviews are part of prominence); the LSA ranking help page (rating and number of reviews); Google's review policy (no incentives, no review gating, no fake engagement).
3. **Reviews and local search (Maps):** what moves rankings in practice: count, rating, recency, steady pace, replies, review text that mentions case types and cities. Field experience labelled as such.
4. **Reviews and LSAs:** how rating and count affect LSA position and lead volume. Ties back to the "ahead" rule in the city-opportunity analysis (higher rating, or the same rating with more reviews).
5. **What gets reviews filtered or a profile penalized:** batch requests, the same device or Wi-Fi, incentives, gating, review swaps, buying reviews. The FTC's rule on fake reviews and testimonials (in effect since October 2024) also applies.
6. **Strategies for injury firms:** when to ask (case milestones, not only settlement), who asks (attorney vs intake), how to ask (direct review link, text and email), steady pace, replying to every review, and handling negative reviews (including reporting ones that break Google's policies).
7. **Proof:** review growth versus LSA impressions and leads for a client (connector data plus a screenshot; consent; "Results vary").
8. **This week:** three actions. **Offer:** an audit focused on reviews and the LSA profile. **Invite:** the webinar after that.

**Compliance:** state bar rules on testimonials differ by state (no legal advice; "we review your state's rules on the call"). Never suggest incentives or gating. No guaranteed results.
