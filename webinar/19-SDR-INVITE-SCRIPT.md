# SDR call script: webinar invite (before each webinar)

**Who calls:** the Upwork SDR (see 18-UPWORK-SDR-JOB-POST.md). **When:** Nov 3-16 for webinar #1, Nov 23-Dec 7 for webinar #2, 10 am-5 pm in the firm's time zone.
**Who we call:** firms from the Instantly list that opened or clicked, firms that engaged with our ads, and past contacts. Never: Florida firms, anyone on the Do Not Contact tab, cell phones (firm main lines only).
**Log every call** on the SDR Call Log tab of the Webinar Contacts sheet, the same day.

## The goal of the call
Get the owner or managing partner registered for the webinar ($20). Second-best: their email, so we can send the link.

## 1. Receptionist
> "Hi, this is {name} with Sutton Injury Law Marketing Group. Gabriel Sutton is hosting a short webinar for personal injury firms on Google Local Services Ads on Tuesday, November 17th, and I'm calling to invite the managing partner. Is {name / the managing partner} available for 30 seconds?"

- **"What's it regarding?"** → "It's an invitation to a webinar on Google Local Services Ads for injury firms. It takes 30 seconds."
- **"Send an email."** → "Happy to. What's the best address for {name}? I'll put 'LSA webinar, Nov 17' in the subject so it's easy to find." Log the email; Outcome = Interested, send info.
- **"They're not available."** → "When's a better time to catch them?" Log it; Outcome = Call back.
- **"We're not interested."** → "Understood, thank you." Outcome = Not interested.

## 2. Decision-maker
> "Hi {first name}, {name} with Sutton Injury Law Marketing Group. I'll be quick. Gabriel Sutton has run Google Local Services Ads for injury firms for 15 years, and on Tuesday, November 17th at 3 pm Eastern he's doing a one-hour webinar on what actually moves firms up in LSAs, including what Google doesn't publish. It's $20 and includes the replay. Can I send you the link?"

- **Yes** → confirm email, send the Eventbrite link right after the call, Outcome = Registered / booked (if they register on the call) or Interested, send info.
- **"Is this a sales pitch?"** → "It's a teaching session with real dashboards from injury firms. At the end Gabriel mentions an audit for firms that want one, but most of the hour is what to do yourself."
- **"We already have an agency."** → "That's common. A lot of firms come to compare what their agency is doing. Want the link?"
- **"We don't run LSAs."** → "Then it's a good hour to decide whether to start. Gabriel covers what it takes to rank, so you know before spending."
- **"Why is it $20?"** → "It keeps the room to people who are serious about it, and you get the replay."
- **"Not a good time."** → "When should I call back?" Outcome = Call back.
- **"Take me off your list."** → "Done, sorry for the interruption." Add them to Do Not Contact the same day. Outcome = Do not call.

## 3. Voicemail (once per firm, max)
> "Hi {first name}, {name} with Sutton Injury Law Marketing Group. Gabriel Sutton is hosting a webinar on Google Local Services Ads for injury firms on Tuesday, November 17th at 3 pm Eastern. I'll email you the link. If you'd like to talk, I'm at {number}. Thanks."

## Rules
- Manual dialing only; no texts unless the person gave consent.
- Two attempts per firm per week, maximum. One voicemail per firm.
- Never promise results, rankings or lead numbers. Never quote prices for services; "Gabriel covers that on the audit call."
- No legal advice. Bar-rule questions: "Gabriel can cover that on a call."
