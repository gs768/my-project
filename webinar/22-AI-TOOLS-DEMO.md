# The three AI tools: what they do and how we show them

**Today:** each tool runs as an AI session our team gives a directive to; the work gets done and the output comes back. There is no app to click through, so the webinar shows **inputs and outputs** on designed slides (`ai-tools`, `ai-citation`, `ai-leadrating`, `ai-autoresponder` in the deck). All sample data on those slides is a fictional firm ("Example Injury Law, Atlanta") and labeled "Illustrative example, not client data."
**How to say it on the webinar:** "These run as AI sessions our team directs. I'll show you what goes in and what comes out." Never call them software products or imply a self-serve app.

## 1. Citation planner
| | |
|---|---|
| **In** | Firm name, office address, phone, website; practice area; market (city); the top 3 LSA firms in that market |
| **What it does** | Checks where the firm and the top 3 are listed (legal directories such as Avvo, Justia, Super Lawyers, the state bar; general ones such as Apple Business Connect, BBB, Yelp, Bing); flags missing listings and wrong details (old address, old phone); ranks the gaps by how many of the top 3 have them |
| **Out** | A prioritized plan: directory, the firm's status, how many of the top 3 have it, cost, next step |
| **The manual part (the hook)** | For paid directories, our team contacts each site for its price. Google doesn't publish this, and agencies rarely do it |

## 2. LSA lead rating
| | |
|---|---|
| **In** | One LSA lead (call recording/transcript or message), time, lead type; the firm's case-type rules and service area |
| **What it does** | Pulls out case type, liability signals, treatment, date of incident and location; scores the lead 1-10 |
| **Out** | Score and label, the case details, the next step (call back within 15 minutes, route to an attorney, or deprioritize), and what to mark in the LSA dashboard (rating, booked). Low-scoring leads that look invalid (job seekers, solicitors, wrong case type) get flagged to check whether Google credited them automatically |

## 3. Autoresponder + image publisher
| | |
|---|---|
| **In** | New LSA messages; the firm's intake rules (hours, case types, who calls back); photos from the firm's phones |
| **Autoresponder out** | A reply within a minute, any hour: empathetic, no legal advice, offers a callback window, texting only with consent; the lead goes into the intake queue |
| **Image publisher out** | Photos checked for being real (camera data intact, not stock), captions written, a schedule of about 2 photos a week to the LSA profile and Google Business Profile |

## Guardrails (say these if asked)
- No legal advice from the autoresponder; an attorney calls back.
- Texting only with consent.
- Client data stays private; demo slides use fictional data.
- No guaranteed results.
