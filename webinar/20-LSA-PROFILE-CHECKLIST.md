## LSA Profile Checklist for Personal Injury Firms
*15 checks to run on your Google Local Services Ads profile this week*

**What Google says it ranks on**
- [ ] **Google Screened badge** is active, and every attorney's license and background check is current
- [ ] **Bid strategy** matches your goal (maximize leads vs. set cost per lead), and the budget isn't running out before the end of the day
- [ ] **Star rating** compared with the top 3 firms showing in your main city
- [ ] **Number of reviews** compared with those same 3 firms
- [ ] **Response time:** you answer LSA calls and messages within minutes, not hours

**What Google doesn't publish (from years of running LSAs)**
- [ ] **Proximity:** where your office is versus where your best cases come from
- [ ] **Business hours** in the profile match when someone actually answers the phone
- [ ] **Complaints:** no unresolved serious or repeated complaints tied to your profile

**Your profile**
- [ ] **Case types** selected match the cases you want (and exclude the ones you don't)
- [ ] **Service area** covers the cities you want, and nothing you can't serve
- [ ] **Photos** are real photos of your team and office, not stock images
- [ ] **Business name** matches your real-world name (no added keywords)

**Your leads**
- [ ] **Last month's charged leads** reviewed: which ones Google credited automatically, and which still look invalid
- [ ] **Answer rate** on LSA calls checked (missed calls cost you leads and ranking)
- [ ] **Review requests** go out steadily after case milestones, not in batches
