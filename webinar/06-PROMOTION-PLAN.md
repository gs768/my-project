## Phase A: Warm-up ads (start now, run 3–4 weeks before the webinar ads)
**Goal:** build pixel data, video-view and engagement audiences from the 4,000-lawyer custom audience, so the paid webinar ads go to people who already know your face.
- Campaign 1, *Video views* (cheapest reach): 3–5 short educational clips from the avatar (below). ~$10–20/day.
- Campaign 2, *Traffic/engagement* to 1–2 useful blog posts (e.g., "What Google says ranks Local Services Ads"). Fire the pixel `ViewContent`.
- Build these audiences as you go: 50%+ video viewers (30/90d), site visitors (90d), page/IG engagers (90d).
- Note: Meta has no formal "warm-up". What helps is pixel history and having warm retargeting pools at launch. This plan gives you both.

## AI avatar videos (ElevenLabs)
- Script → your cloned voice in ElevenLabs → avatar video. 30–45 sec, captioned, vertical 9:16 plus a 1:1 version.
- **Warm-up series (no pitch):** 1) "Citations aren't the LSA secret." 2) "Google ranks you on how fast you answer." 3) "You can get credit for bad LSA leads." 4) "Why cheap city pages hurt PI firms now." 5) "The review mistake after every big settlement."
- **Webinar series:** 1) Invitation (what you'll learn, $20). 2) "Here's a screenshot from Google…" teaser. 3) Client-result teaser (with permission). 4) Last call.
- **Disclosure:** Meta requires an AI label on realistic AI-generated video/voice of a person. Turn on the "AI info" label. It's also more honest with a lawyer audience. Record the final invitation as a real 20-second selfie video so it carries more trust.

## Event listings (free reach plus credibility)
| Platform | Use | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Eventbrite** | List the $20 event. It can **replace the Gravity Forms + PayPal checkout**: built-in payment, a Zoom integration that sends the join link automatically, reminders, and discovery search | Free listing; ticket fee ~$1–2 (pass it to the buyer) |
| Facebook Event (on the page) | Ads can promote the event; "Interested" builds an audience | Free |
| LinkedIn Event (Gabriel's profile + company page) | Invite PI attorney connections directly | Free |
| AllEvents / Luma | Extra discovery and indexing | Free |
| PI attorney Facebook/LinkedIn groups | Post a value post, link in comments, follow group rules | Free |
| Email signature + website banner | Every outgoing email | Free |

**Recommendation:** use Eventbrite as the registration + payment + Zoom-link system for Webinar #1. It's faster than wiring Gravity Forms + PayPal + Zapier, and it's free for you. Export attendees to the Google Sheet / Mailchimp after each event. Keep the WordPress page as the landing page with an embedded Eventbrite checkout, so the pixel still fires.
