# Visual style and production (inspiration: Justia's "Google PPC & LSAs" webinar)

Reference: https://onward.justia.com/justia-webinars-google-advertising-ppc-lsas/ (64-minute recording, posted on their blog and YouTube). We borrow the **look and structure only**. No copying of their logo, colors as a brand match, text or slides.

## What their production does (from Gabriel's screenshots)
| Element | What it looks like | Use for us |
|---|---|---|
| Branded frame | Deep blue gradient background around everything; logo in a corner | Our new brand colors + Sutton Injury Law Marketing Group logo (from Raghu's media package) |
| Agenda / section slides | Split layout: light panel on the left with the section title, dark panel on the right with the agenda bullets; **the current section is highlighted** | Reuse one agenda slide as the divider for every section, highlighting where we are |
| Content slides | Light background, few words, large type, small logo bottom-left | Same |
| Presenter camera | Picture-in-picture in the **bottom-right** with a name tag | Leave the bottom-right ~20% of every content slide empty for Gabriel's camera |
| Data slides | One idea per slide, e.g. a simple formula ("Total connected calls from charged leads ÷ total calls received") in big text | Our KPIs: impressions, charged leads, cost per lead, CPM, one per slide |
| Name tags (lower thirds) | White rounded bars with each speaker's name under their camera | "Gabriel Sutton, Founder" |
| Q&A segment | The audience question as a **banner across the top**, speakers side by side underneath | Shereesa reads the question; it shows as a banner over Gabriel's camera |
| Replay | YouTube with **chapters** ("Avoiding Common Pitfalls", etc.), embedded in a blog post with a newsletter sign-up under it | Phase C: replay on YouTube with chapters, embedded in a blog post on our site, with an email sign-up |

## Can Zoom plus a slide deck do this?
Mostly, yes, with two caveats:
- **Picture-in-picture over slides:** Zoom's **"Slides as virtual background"** (share screen, Advanced) puts Gabriel's camera over the slides. That's the core of their look, and free in our Zoom plan.
- **Name tags and question banners** are not standard Zoom features. Options, cheapest first:
  1. **Bake them into the slides** (a name tag on the title slide; a Q&A slide template with a blank banner Sam types the question into). Free, works today.
  2. **OBS Studio** (free) as a virtual camera into Zoom: full branded frame, lower thirds, banners. More setup; needs a rehearsal.
  3. A streaming studio tool (e.g. StreamYard or Riverside). Polished, but a new subscription. I can't tell from the screenshots which tool Justia used.
- **Recommendation:** Zoom + "slides as virtual background" + a slide template with the name tag and Q&A banner built in. Revisit OBS for webinar #2 if the first one goes well.

## Slide template to build (Gabriel makes the slides; this is the spec)
1. **Title slide:** webinar title, date, Gabriel's photo/name tag, logo.
2. **Agenda/divider slide** (used 6 times, highlighting the current section): The hook · What Google says · What Google punishes · The 3-phase system · Proof (case studies + live demo) · Q&A.
3. **Content slide:** light background, headline + up to 3 short lines or one screenshot; logo bottom-left; **bottom-right 20% empty** for the camera.
4. **Screenshot slide:** Google's page with the URL visible and one highlight box.
5. **Data slide:** one number or one formula, very large.
6. **Case-study slide:** before / after table (4 rows max) + the one-line takeaway + "Results vary."
7. **Q&A slide:** banner at the top for the question, empty area for the camera.
8. **Offer slide:** "LSA Market Audit": what it is, limited weekly slots, booking link/QR code.
9. **Closing slide:** thank you, replay note, booking link again.
Colors and fonts: from the new brand package; use one accent color only for the highlighted agenda item and the key numbers.

## Rehearsal checks for the look
- [ ] Camera framed so Gabriel fits the bottom-right box without covering text
- [ ] Slides readable on a phone (big type, under ~20 words per slide)
- [ ] Highlight color visible in both bright and dim rooms
- [ ] The Q&A banner text is large enough to read in the recording
