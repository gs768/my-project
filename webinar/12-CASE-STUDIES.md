## Case study 1: OSP Cleveland (Obral Silk & Pal), "same budget, 2.5x the visibility"
**When it started doing well:** the daily data shows a first lift on **Sep 2, 2025** and a clear step-change on **Sep 20-21, 2025**.
- Aug 10-31: about **61 impressions/day** (and close to zero Aug 28-31, as the monthly budget ran out)
- Sep 1-19: about **87/day**
- Sep 20-30: about **231/day** (about 3.8x August)

**Monthly: 4 months before, then after**
| Month | Impressions | Spend | Charged leads | Cost per lead | CPM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| May 2025 | 2,223 | $6,472 | 29 | $223 | $2,911 |
| Jun 2025 | 1,857 | $6,500 | 27 | $241 | $3,500 |
| Jul 2025 | 1,861 | $6,499 | 26 | $250 | $3,492 |
| Aug 2025 | 1,891 | $6,298 | 26 | $242 | $3,330 |
| **Sep 2025** | **4,190** | $6,896 | 27 | $255 | **$1,646** |
| **Oct 2025** | **4,773** | $6,692 | 26 | $257 | **$1,402** |
| Nov 2025 | 3,240 | $5,804 | 23 | $252 | $1,791 |
| Dec 2025 | 1,673 | $6,514 | 22 | $296 | $3,894 |
- **The win:** at roughly the same budget, impressions rose from about 1,890 to **4,190 and 4,773** (+122% / +152%), and **CPM fell 58%** ($3,330 to $1,402).
- **What it is not:** charged leads stayed at about 26-27 and cost per lead at about $250. The account was **budget-capped** (it ran out at the end of August), so more visibility couldn't turn into more paid leads without a bigger budget. In 2026, when spend rose to about $9.7k, leads rose to 37-41 a month, which supports the "raise the budget into the visibility" point.
- **Honest caveat:** the lift faded by December (1,673). Explain that, or end the story at November.
