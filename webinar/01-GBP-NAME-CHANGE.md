# Google Business Profile: renaming to "Sutton Injury Law Marketing Group"

**Start here. Steps 1–2 can take weeks, and nothing in GBP should change until they're done.**

## The office problem (read first)

Google's guidelines only allow a GBP for businesses that **meet customers in person**, either at a staffed location or by traveling to the customer. With no office:

| Your situation | What Google allows |
|---|---|
| You (or staff) visit law firms in person (audits, onboarding, filming the client videos) | ✅ **Service-area business (SAB):** hide the address, list the states/cities you serve. Verification uses your home address, but it's never shown. |
| Everything is remote (Zoom/phone only) | ❌ Technically ineligible. A name change triggers a fresh review and could get the profile **suspended** instead of renamed. |
| Virtual office / mailbox / unstaffed coworking address | ❌ Not allowed. Google catches these during video verification. |

**Recommendation:** if you do meet firms in person (and you can make it true, e.g. on-site intake audits and video shoots, which fit this offer anyway), convert to an SAB *at the same time* as the rename. Then there's only one re-verification. If it's 100% remote, keep the current profile untouched, because a rename puts it at risk, and lean on the website, LinkedIn and the seminar for trust.

## Step-by-step

**1. Make the name legally real (1–4 weeks)**
- File a **DBA / assumed name** "Sutton Injury Law Marketing Group" for your existing LLC in its state of formation (or amend the LLC name). Some states also require newspaper publication, which adds 2–6 weeks.
- Update the EIN record if the legal name changes (IRS Form 8822-B, or a letter). A DBA alone doesn't need this.
- Business bank account: add the DBA so you can take seminar payments under the new name.

**2. Build the proof Google will ask for (in parallel)**
- DBA certificate / state filing showing the new name.
- An invoice or bank statement with the new name **and** your verification address.
- The website live with the new name, logo, footer and schema (already planned).
- For video verification, have ready: a branded item (laptop sticker, printed letterhead, signed client agreement on new letterhead), your business license / DBA, and the area outside showing your street sign. One continuous video, no cuts.

**3. Align the citations (1–2 weeks, BrightLocal can do this)**
Update the name on Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook, LinkedIn, Clutch, Yelp, BBB, and the Citation Builder. Google trusts a name it sees consistently elsewhere.

**4. Make the GBP edit (when 1–3 are done)**
- In the GBP edit screen, change **Business name** to `Sutton Injury Law Marketing Group`. No keywords, no "| PI Marketing" (keyword stuffing gets profiles suspended).
- Same session: **Location → turn off "Show business address to customers"**, add service areas (up to 20 states/metros; exclude FL to match the offer).
- Category: keep `Marketing agency`; add `Internet marketing service` as secondary.
- Expect a **"Changes under review"** status and possibly a re-verification request (usually video). Typical time is 3 days to 3 weeks.

**5. If it gets suspended or stuck**
File the reinstatement form with the DBA, invoice and website. Don't create a new profile, because duplicates make it worse. Reviews stay attached to the profile through a rename.
